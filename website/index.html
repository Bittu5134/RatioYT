Built for cleaner browsing.

Simple, thoughtful features designed to restore honesty to YouTube recommendations.

Community ratings

Powered by the Return YouTube Dislike API. Shows accurate, community-collected rating ratios on feed cards, search results, shorts, and playlist queues.

Four score styles

Display scores however you prefer: classic percentages (96%), 10-point decimals (9.6 / 10), simple smiley emojis (😊 / 😐 / 😞), or 5-star SVGs with half stars.

Click-to-reveal blur

Low-scoring clickbait videos below your chosen threshold are blurred automatically. Hover to see the subtle peek hint, click to reveal, and move away to re-blur.

No hover autoplay

The blur overlay sits on top of the card and blocks pointer events from reaching YouTube's thumbnail. Hovering over a hidden video will never trigger noisy autoplay previews.

Local IndexedDB cache

Fetched scores are cached in your browser's IndexedDB for 7 days. Repeated views load instantly with zero extra API network requests.

Private and lightweight

Zero trackers, zero analytics, zero external dependencies. The extension only contacts the Return YouTube Dislike API to lookup scores.

Frequently asked questions.

Where do the like and dislike numbers come from?

RatioYT queries the public Return YouTube Dislike API, which calculates ratings from archived dislike counts before YouTube removed them and continuous community contribution data.

Why does the blur overlay prevent video autoplay?

When a low-rated video is hidden, RatioYT places an overlay over the thumbnail with pointer capture. Mouse events hit our overlay instead of YouTube's thumbnail, preventing YouTube's preview player from auto-starting.

How does unblurring work?

Hovering over a hidden video keeps it blurred and displays a subtle eye icon. Clicking the card unblurs it so you can see it while hovering. When your mouse leaves the card, it re-blurs automatically.

Does RatioYT collect any personal data?

None. RatioYT contains zero analytics, zero trackers, and zero telemetry. It only makes requests to fetch video scores and caches them in your local browser IndexedDB.
